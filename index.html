Tom & Jerry — The Missing Cheese Mystery

The Scenario

One morning, Tom prepares a delicious piece of cheese for his lunch.
He places the cheese on the kitchen table and goes to get a plate.
When Tom returns ...

THE CHEESE IS GONE!

Tom immediately sees Jerry running away with the cheese.
Tom thinks:
"JERRY STOLE MY CHEESE!"

Tom starts chasing Jerry around the house.

But the Lean problem solver stops him and asks:
"WAIT! Jerry taking the cheese is what happened ... but WHY did the cheese disappear?"

And this is where our Why-Why investigation begins!
